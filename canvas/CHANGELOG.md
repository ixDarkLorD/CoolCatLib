# Changelog
This file is for listing all the changes to this project

## v26.1.2-7 Release | Oct 5, 2026
### ✨ New Features
- Camera shake (`api.client.camera.CameraShake`): `CameraShake.shake(degrees, ticks)` for a jolt, `shake(origin, radius, degrees, ticks)` for one that is strongest at a place and gone at its radius, and `add(new Shake(...))` for a curve of the mod's own (a swell, a rumble). Shakes add up, are capped, and follow the game's "Distortion Effects" accessibility slider; `CameraShake.setScale` is for a mod's own option. From the server: `api.vfx.ShakeControl.shake(level, origin, radius, degrees, ticks)` or `shake(player, degrees, ticks)`.
- Camera zoom (`api.client.camera.CameraZoom`): `CameraZoom.set(factor)` narrows the view smoothly, as through a real lens, and slows the mouse by as much so aiming stays steady; `reset()` puts it back, and `ease(perTick)` sets how heavy the lens feels. `CameraZoom.scroll(active, min, max, step)` hands the mouse wheel to the zoom while a condition holds (a lens raised), leaving the hotbar alone.
- Scene viewports (`api.client.viewport.SceneViewport`): a small 3D scene of blocks, whole structures and entities drawn to a texture of its own, from a camera that circles it and can spin. It is drawn by the game's own renderers with the game's own UI lighting, and only when its scene or camera changes (or a set number of times a second while it moves). Show it in a screen with `SceneViewportWidget` (drag to turn, scroll to zoom), or on any face in the world through `texture()`.
- HUD takeover (`api.client.gui.HudTakeover`): `HudTakeover.register(id, active, view)` hides the game's HUD while a condition holds and draws a view of the mod's own in its place: a scope, a lens, a visor.
- Bloom, with no assets: `ScreenEffects.bloom(id, intensity).enable()` makes bright parts of the frame glow into what is around them, so world effects, fire and lava read as light. It is a screen effect like any other (fades, strength, conditions, `enableFor` for a surge). The definition is `coolcatcanvas:bloom` (`ScreenEffects.BLOOM`), with the uniforms `BLOOM_THRESHOLD`, `BLOOM_INTENSITY` and `BLOOM_RADIUS`.
- Shader materials for world effects: `VfxMaterial.withShader(id)` draws a material with a fragment shader of the mod's own (`assets/<namespace>/shaders/<path>.fsh`) in place of the textured one, for what a texture cannot do. The shader gets the quad's UVs and colour, the scene's depth and `coolcatcanvas:vfx.glsl`. `VfxDraw.quad(a, b, c, d, u0, v0, u1, v1, argb)` takes any UV range, which is how a shape passes its own numbers to its shader.
- Rifts (`api.client.vfx.WorldRifts`, `RiftSpec`, `Rift`): a tear in the air drawn by a built-in shader (`VfxMaterial.RIFT_SHADER`) and run through three acts. It opens with a flash, its opening sound and a shake of the camera nearby, overshooting and settling; while open its edge crawls and crackles, a starry elsewhere drifts inside it, motes are drawn into it and its ambient sound loops; it closes to a line and snaps shut with a ring of light and its closing sound. `WorldRifts.open(spec, position)` stays until `rift.close()`; `open(spec, position, ticks)` closes by itself. A `RiftSpec` sets size, colour, timings, the three sounds, the motes and the shake. From the server: `api.vfx.RiftControl.open(level, spec id, position[, ticks])` and `close(level, id)` for rifts the clients registered with `WorldRifts.register`; `RiftControl.DEFAULT` needs no registering.
- Cutscenes (`api.cutscene`, `api.client.cutscene`): a camera flown along keyframes while the player watches. A `Cutscene` is plain data, the same on both sides: built in code (`Cutscene.builder()`), read from `assets/<namespace>/cutscene/<path>.json`, made in the in-game editor, or sent whole by the server.
  - Keyframes (`CameraKeyframe`): a time, position, yaw, pitch, roll (a dutch angle) and field of view (zoom), each reached along a smooth curve through its neighbours, a straight line or a cut (`Interpolation`), paced by any `Easing`.
  - Around the camera: letterbox bars framing an aspect ratio (`letterbox()`, 2.39 by default) that slide in and out, fades to any colour (`fadeIn`, `fadeOut`, or a `ValueTrack` for fades in the middle), subtitles, and markers that fire `CutsceneEvents.MARKER` on a beat.
  - `CameraFocus` keeps the camera looking at a point, an entity or the player for a stretch, turning to it and back; `blendIn` / `blendOut` glide from and to the player's own view; `CutsceneAnchor` measures a cutscene from the world or from the player, so one orbit plays anywhere.
  - Playing (`Cutscenes.play(id)` or `play(cutscene)`, `playAt(...)` for an origin of its own): hides the HUD and hand, draws the player's body, and holds the player still, each by the cutscene's own settings. The `CutscenePlayback` it returns can pause, seek, change speed, stop, and tell when it ends. Players skip a skippable one by holding a key (Enter by default); pausing the game pauses it.
  - From the server (`CutsceneControl`): `play(player, id)`, `play(player, id, cutscene)` to send one the client doesn't have, `playAt`, `stop`, and `ServerCutsceneEvents`: `STARTED`, `TICK` (every server tick a player watches), `KEYFRAME` and `MARKER` (as the player's client reaches them) and `ENDED` with how it ended (finished, skipped, stopped, replaced, left).
  - Events (`CutsceneEvents`, client): `BEFORE_START` (cancellable), `STARTED`, `TICK` while it plays, `KEYFRAME` as the camera reaches each one, `MARKER`, `SUBTITLE`, `PAUSED`, `CAMERA` (every frame, may hand back a changed camera), `BEFORE_SKIP` (cancellable) and `ENDED` with the reason.
  - `editable(false)` keeps a mod's cutscene out of the in-game editor; registered from code, it can't be replaced by a resource pack either.
  - `Letterbox.show(aspect, ticks)` / `hide(ticks)` for bars without a cutscene.
  - An in-game editor (`Cutscenes.openEditor()`, a key unbound by default, and `/coolcatcanvas_client cutscene editor` in development environments): fly to a spot and press K to drop a keyframe, with the path drawn in the world (a dot a tick, so spacing shows speed). Its screen has a timeline to scrub and drag keyframes on, the selected keyframe's numbers, easing and path, the scene's letterbox, blends, fades and end hold, a live preview through the camera at the playhead, and Play. It saves JSON to `config/coolcatcanvas/cutscenes/`, playable as `coolcatcanvas:saved/<name>` and ready to copy into a resource pack. Focuses, subtitles and markers are kept when a cutscene is loaded but are edited in the file.
- OBJ models (`api.client.model.ObjModel`, `ObjModels`): Wavefront `.obj` files drawn in the world by the game's own renderers, so they are lit, fogged and sorted as anything else. `ObjModels.get(id)` reads any file under `assets/` (kept, and read again when resource packs reload); `ObjModel.parse(text)` reads one from anywhere. Faces of any number of corners, negative indices, missing texture coordinates and missing normals (a flat one is worked out) are all read; `o`, `g` and `usemtl` split a model into named parts, each of which can be drawn by itself with its own texture, tint, light and pose. `model.submit(pose, collector, renderType, light)` from an entity or block entity renderer, or `render(pose, vertexConsumer, ...)` into any buffer, with the entity render types (`entityCutout`, `entityTranslucent`, `eyes`). Materials (`.mtl`) are not read: the caller picks the texture.
- `api.utils.Easings`: the easing curves by name (`byName`, `nameOf`, `CODEC`, `register` for a mod's own), for files, commands and packets.
- More `Easing` curves: `SMOOTH`, `QUART_IN` / `OUT` / `IN_OUT`, `EXPO_IN_OUT`, `BACK_IN`, `BACK_IN_OUT`, `ELASTIC_OUT` and `BOUNCE_OUT`.

### 🔧 Changes
- Everything that animates by the clock stops while the game is paused and goes on from where it stopped: world effects (`VfxFrame.time()`, the `Time` of world-effect shaders, rifts), skyboxes and screen effects (their `time`, `deltaTime`, ages, fades and the `Time` of their shaders). A paused game is a still picture, and nothing has jumped ahead when it is taken up again. World particles also hold their exact place while paused (they used to be drawn at the end of their tick). A game counts as paused only where the game says so: a singleplayer world with a pausing screen open.
- `VfxMaterial` has a fourth component, `shader` (null for the built-in one). The three-argument constructor and the `additive` / `alpha` / `luminous` factories are unchanged.

## v26.1.2-6 Release | Oct 5, 2026
### ✨ New Features
- World effects (`api.client.vfx`): glowing shapes drawn in the world, after the whole level and before the hand, lit by nothing. They hide behind solid things and fade softly where they meet them (`VfxMaterial.softness`), so nothing ends in a hard line at a wall or the ground.
  - Materials (`VfxMaterial`): a texture, a blend (`VfxBlend.ADDITIVE` for light, `ALPHA` for smoke, `LUMINOUS` for black-background glow textures against bright skies) and a softness. Everything of one material in a frame is one draw call.
  - Effect particles (`ParticleSpec`, `WorldParticles`): size, opacity and spin follow three-point eased curves (`Curve`) over each particle's life, colour shifts around the colour wheel so gradients stay vivid, lifetimes are randomised, with gravity, drag and velocity-stretched sparks (`Shape.SPARK`). `WorldParticles.spawn`, `burst` and `line`. They are not vanilla particles: nothing to register, nothing on the server.
  - Shapes every frame (`WorldVfx.register` with a `VfxRenderer`): camera-facing billboards, beams, flat ground quads, free quads, and ribbons that follow a `Trail`.
  - One-liners that draw themselves and fade: `WorldVfx.beam`, `flash` and `groundFlash`.
  - Built-in textures: `VfxMaterial.GLOW`, `SPARKLE`, `STAR`, `RING`, `SMOKE` and `STREAK` (white, with brightness in alpha, so they take any colour and blend either way).
  - From the server (`api.vfx.VfxControl`): `VfxControl.play(level, id, VfxParams)` plays an effect a client registered with `VfxEffects.register`, as one small packet to each player in range.
  - Shaders can import `coolcatcanvas:vfx.glsl` for the camera block and the depth-fade helpers.

## v26.1.2-5 Release | Oct 4, 2026
### 🔧 Changes
- Renamed `ScreenEffectStage` to `ScreenEffectScope`, and `ScreenEffect.stage()` / `stage(...)` to `scope()` / `scope(...)`: `.scope(ScreenEffectScope.SCREEN)` puts an effect over the HUD and menus. Mods calling the old names need updating. The effects screen's `screen_effect.coolcatcanvas.screen.stage.*` translation keys are now `...screen.scope.*`.

## v26.1.2-4 Release | Oct 4, 2026
### ✨ New Features
- A built-in tint screen effect, with nothing to set up: `ScreenEffects.tint(id, argb)` makes a color wash under a mod's own id the first time and recolors it after, so `ScreenEffects.tint(id, 0x59FF0000).enableFor(5)` is a red flash wherever it's called. It's a screen effect like any other (fades, strength, conditions), over the world or, on the `SCREEN` stage, over the HUD and menus too. From the server, `ScreenEffectControl.tint(player, id, argb[, ticks])` does the same for a player, with nothing registered on the client. The definition is `coolcatcanvas:tint` (`ScreenEffects.TINT`, color uniform `TINT_COLOR`).

## v26.1.2-1 Release | Sep 30, 2026 (Minecraft 26.1.2)
### ✨ New Features
- Runs on Minecraft 26.1, 26.1.1 and 26.1.2 (NeoForge 26.1.0.19-beta or newer, Fabric API 0.145.1 or newer).
- First release: the render and visuals half of what used to be CoolCatLib, split out and built on CoolCatLib: Core. Everything below was moved from CoolCatLib into the `net.ixdarklord.coolcatcanvas` package and `coolcatcanvas` namespace.
- Screen effects (`api.client.effect`): full-screen post-processing shaders whose uniforms can change every frame (vanilla's post chains bake them in at load).
  - `ScreenEffects.register(id, definition)` from a vanilla-format `post_effect` JSON (vanilla's own included) or a `ScreenEffectDefinition` built in code; any number stack, ordered by `priority`, drawn over the world (`WORLD`) or over everything including the HUD and menus (`SCREEN`).
  - Smooth fades (`fade`, `Easing`), timed pulses (`enableFor`), animated strength, conditions (`activeWhen`), strength functions of the frame (`strength`) and per-frame callbacks (`onFrame`).
  - Uniforms by name: set, animated (`animateTo`) or bound to a function of the frame; `autoBlend` fades shaders that ignore the effect's strength.
  - Shaders get `Strength`, `Time`, `Age` and `Seed` plus noise and luminance helpers from `#moj_import <coolcatcanvas:screen_effect.glsl>`; persistent targets allow feedback effects; reloaded with resource packs; a failing effect is logged and skipped.
  - No effects of its own: mods bring their own `post_effect` JSON and shaders, or use vanilla's (`minecraft:invert`, ...).
  - `api.effect.ScreenEffectControl` drives a player's effects from the server.
  - Layers (`ScreenEffects.layers()`): the draw order of every effect, from priorities until rearranged (move up/down, to a layer, to the top or bottom, reset).
  - An effects screen (`ScreenEffects.openScreen()`, a key unbound by default, and `/coolcatcanvas_client effects` in development environments only): every selectable effect with a switch, the active ones as drag-and-drop layers grouped by stage with a strength slider each, a live unblurred preview and a Preview mode hiding the panels. Players' choices (on/off, strengths, order) are saved to `config/coolcatcanvas-screen-effects.json`. Effects have a `displayName`, `description` and `selectable` flag for it.
  - Events (`ScreenEffectEvents`): `BEFORE_TOGGLE` (cancellable) and `TOGGLED` with the cause (code, condition, timeout, player, server), `STRENGTH_CHANGED`, `UNIFORM_CHANGED`, `LAYERS_CHANGED` and `LOADED`.
- Custom skyboxes (`api.client.sky`): layers drawn inside vanilla's sky pass, around the sun, moon and stars.
  - `Skyboxes.register(id, definition)` from a `SkyboxDefinition` built in code or `assets/<namespace>/skybox/<path>.json`; resource packs add skyboxes with no code by listing `dimensions` (dimensions without a sky, like the Nether, get one while it shows).
  - Layer types: `cubemap` (six faces in a 3x2 image), `panorama` (equirectangular), `sprite` (an image placed in the sky, e.g. a planet), `gradient`, and `shader` (your own fragment shader, with `#moj_import <coolcatcanvas:sky.glsl>`); any type can swap in its own fragment shader.
  - Per layer: blend mode (alpha, additive, multiply, screen), tint, orientation, rotation over real time, game time, the day or vanilla's sun/moon/star angle, flipbook animation with optional interpolation, day-time fade windows, rain fade, horizon/underwater fog, and 4 shader params.
  - Hides vanilla's sky color, sunrise, sun, moon, stars, void, End sky and End flashes, fading them out as the skybox fades in.
  - Runtime control: fades, `enableFor`, conditions (`activeWhen`), per-frame callbacks, and per layer (`Skybox.layer(name)`) visibility, animated opacity and tint, and params set, animated or bound to a function of the frame. `api.sky.SkyboxControl` drives them from the server: enable, disable, `toggle`, `setEnabled`, timed pulses and layer changes, for one player or several (`to(players)`, `inLevel(level)`, `everyone(server)`).
  - Cheap: nothing runs while no skybox shows; a visible layer is one draw from a shared static mesh, all layers of a stage share one render pass and one uniform upload.
- `api.utils.Easing`: easing curves.
- GUI components and widgets (`api.client.gui.components`: panels, scroll and drag widgets, slide animations, `ColorableImageButton`), and `api.client.utils` `RenderUtils`, `NineSliceInfo` and `ScreenAnchor`.
  - `ViewportPanel` (pan and zoom) edge fades show where content continues past an edge, and shrink smoothly into their side as the end of the content on that side comes into view.
- Animated color gradients (`api.utils.ColorGradient`): color stops looping around (`ColorGradient.of(colors...)`) or the full rainbow (`RAINBOW`, `rainbow(saturation, brightness)`), scrolling over time (`withSpeed`) and along the text or outline (`withSpread`), with each letter colored (`perLetter(n)`) or the whole text in one cycling color (`wholeText()`, `RAINBOW_WHOLE`). Presets: `RAINBOW`, `RAINBOW_WHOLE`, `PASTEL_RAINBOW`, `FIRE`, `OCEAN`, `AURORA`.
- Gradient components: `gradient.literal("...")`, `gradient.apply(component)` or a style with `gradient.textColor()` make a component animate wherever it's drawn (chat, tooltips, item names, signs, widget labels). In JSON text and commands (`/tellraw`) the color is `"coolcatcanvas:rainbow"`, `"coolcatcanvas:rainbow_whole"`, `"coolcatcanvas:rainbow/<speed>/<spread>/<saturation>/<brightness>"` or `"coolcatcanvas:gradient/<speed>/<spread>/#RRGGBB/#RRGGBB..."`; players without Canvas can't read it.
- Gradient and outlined text (`api.client.utils.TextEffects`): `gradient(text, gradient)` and `rainbow(text)` recolor existing text and stay animated when kept; outlined text in a solid color or a gradient.
- Outlines (`api.client.utils.Outline`): solid or a gradient (`Outline.gradient`, `Outline.rainbow()`), with a thickness and padding, drawn around any rectangle; `ElementOutlines.set(widget, outline[, when])` attaches one to any widget (vanilla's too), optionally only while a condition holds (hovered, focused...).
